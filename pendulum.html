Pendulum

A pendulum riding a cart on a rail. Slide the dashed ghost cart left and right and the real cart chases it — the arm above only ever feels the shove and gravity, so whipping the cart across is the only way to throw it upright. Hand it to the autopilot and it swings itself up, then keeps balancing while it drives to wherever you park the ghost.
