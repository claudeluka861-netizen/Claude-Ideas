Conservation of momentum

Even Split

Two wagons sit coupled on a plank balanced across a fulcrum. Blow the charge between them and they go off like a gunshot — but every force in that bang is internal, so their shared centre of mass cannot move an inch. The heavy one is thrown slowly and stops short, the light one is thrown far, and the plank never notices. Let the brakes bring them to rest and read the two distances off the plank: half the mass, twice the distance.
